Tags help readers discover related content across your blog.

## Adding tags

```yaml
tags:
  - javascript
  - tutorial
  - featured
```

## Tag pages

Each tag gets its own page at `/tags/{tag}/index.html` with paginated post lists.

## Featured tag

Posts tagged `featured` (or your configured `posts.featured_tag`) appear in the homepage featured section.

We are excited to launch **Paper Theme** for Everkm Publish — a port of the beloved Astro Paper visual style and blog features.

## Highlights

- Tailwind CSS v4 design tokens
- Featured posts via the `featured` tag
- Paginated post and tag lists
- Archives grouped by year and month

Stay tuned for more updates!

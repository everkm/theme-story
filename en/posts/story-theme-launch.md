We are excited to launch **Story Theme** for Everkm Publish — a port of the beloved hexo-theme-redefine visual style and blog features.

## Highlights

- Story design tokens with light / dark mode
- Featured posts via the `featured` tag
- Paginated post and tag lists
- Archives grouped by year and month

Stay tuned for more updates!

This is the **about** page for the Story Theme demo site.

Story Theme supports:

- Virtual pages for home, about, posts list, tags, archives, links, and album
- Markdown posts in any content folder
- Dark mode, View Transitions, and Algolia search (when `algolia_search` is configured)
